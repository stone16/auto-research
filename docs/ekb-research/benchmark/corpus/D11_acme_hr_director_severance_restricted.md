# Director Severance Schedule

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

Restricted: executive HR only.

Directors terminated without cause receive severance equal to 1.5x base salary,
paid over 12 months, if they sign the standard release. Vice presidents require
separate board approval and are not covered by this schedule.

This document is intentionally restricted. General HR, sales, ops, and all Globex
principals must not retrieve or cite it.
