# Acme Product Catalog

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

Acme sells three enterprise knowledge products: Atlas Search, Beacon Answers,
and Compass Sync. Atlas Search is the governed retrieval tier. Beacon Answers is
the answer service that requires citations for every customer-facing response.
Compass Sync imports documents from approved SaaS connectors.

| SKU | Product | Primary buyer | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AS-100 | Atlas Search | Platform engineering | Hybrid lexical and vector retrieval |
| BA-200 | Beacon Answers | Support operations | Requires source citation spans |
| CS-300 | Compass Sync | IT operations | Uses connector-specific retention rules |

Sales teams must not promise cross-tenant search. Cross-workspace sharing needs
a written grant before a customer reference can be reused.
