# Vendor Onboarding Checklist

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

Vendors must provide security contact, DPA status, SOC report status, subprocessor
list, and support escalation path. A vendor cannot be marked production-ready
until procurement, security, and the owning workspace approve the record.

The checklist is internal to Acme ops. Cross-tenant reuse requires a named shared
grant tied to a specific document, not a broad workspace grant.
