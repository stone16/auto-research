# HR Onboarding SOP

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

1. Create the employee identity in the tenant directory.
2. Add the employee to the correct workspace groups.
3. Grant temporary access to onboarding documents for 14 days.
4. Remove onboarding-only grants after the manager confirms completion.
5. Record every access-grant change in the audit log.

The onboarding group may read benefits, travel, and equipment documents. It may
not read restricted executive compensation documents.
