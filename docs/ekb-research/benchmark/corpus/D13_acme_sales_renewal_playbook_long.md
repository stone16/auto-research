# Renewal Playbook Long Form

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

Renewal conversations should begin 120 days before contract end. The account team
must review product usage, unresolved support issues, executive sponsor status,
and any security questionnaire blockers. Multi-hop renewal answers often require
combining product catalog, discount policy, and support incident history.

Section A: Account health

Healthy accounts have weekly active users above 60 percent of licensed seats,
search success above 82 percent, and fewer than three unresolved critical support
issues. Accounts below any threshold need a recovery plan before pricing is sent.

Section B: Expansion signals

Expansion signals include repeated requests for new connectors, more than two
workspaces asking for governed answers, and audit teams asking for trace_id
exports. Expansion notes must stay inside the tenant workspace.

Section C: Renewal risks

Risk notes must separate sourced facts from account-manager judgment. If no
permitted document supports a claim, the renewal assistant must say no permitted
source is available.

Section D: Approval path

Discounts above 18 percent require finance and legal approval. Product security
exceptions require the security review process and cannot be traded for price.
