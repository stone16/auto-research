# Sales Discount Policy

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

Standard account executives may approve discounts up to 12 percent. Regional
sales managers may approve discounts up to 18 percent. Finance and legal must
approve any discount above 18 percent or any discount tied to data-processing
exceptions.

Discount notes belong in the account workspace. They must not include HR data,
customer secrets, or competitor-specific margin floors from another tenant.
