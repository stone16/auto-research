# Benefits FAQ

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

Q: When does health coverage begin?
A: Coverage begins on the first day of the month after the employee start date.

Q: Who may see dependent information?
A: HR operations and the employee may see dependent information. Sales and ops
workspaces may not retrieve it.

Q: What is the wellness stipend?
A: Full-time employees receive 85 USD per month for approved wellness expenses.
