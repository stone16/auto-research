# Data Retention Notice ZH EN

Status: active
Date: 2026-06-27

English: Knowledge-base source documents are retained for 180 days after removal
from active use unless a legal hold or customer contract requires a longer
period. Retrieval indexes must stop citing removed documents immediately after a
new index_version is published.

Chinese: 知识库来源文档在停止使用后保留 180 天，除非法律保留或客户合同要求更长
期限。新的 index_version 发布后，旧索引不得继续引用已移除的文档。

The number 180 is the active rule. Older retention documents are superseded.
